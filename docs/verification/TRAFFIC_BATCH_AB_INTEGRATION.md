- **VehicleController:** the existing class is split into a partial curb file; there is no second movement controller. It routes along the forced legal approach edge, brakes before entering the pull-in, follows the authored cubic in ground metres, stops at its actual endpoint/tangent, waits, admits a safe lane merge, follows pull-out and resumes the forced departure edge. Board/Collect never follow from merely passing the access RoadNode. Curb endpoints consume arc travel rather than the old node-arrival snap.
- **Ride demand/DTO/fare:** candidates require valid reachable explicit stops. Stop references and stable stop IDs travel with offer/request snapshots; passenger IDs, offer IDs, expiration, decline cooldown and reserve/release/consume semantics remain. The default interface stop overloads fail closed for old mocks/implementations; production implements them. Legacy node hail/retarget APIs resolve a unique explicit stop through the injected registry or return false. No HUD redesign or new currency name is introduced.

## Motion, cancellation and accounting contracts

Bay leases are separate from junction reservations. A parked bay cannot occupy a node conflict; stop validation covers ordinary intersections, registered signal controllers (including disabled controls), and authored approach controls. All manoeuvre samples clear the conflict circle by the vehicle half-diagonal plus body margin. A committed Batch A turn retains its conflict reservation until its full authored exit/rear clearance. An uncommitted queue never acquires it just because a taxi request is active.

A cancelled logical ride immediately becomes Idle and pays nothing. Physical movement with a stop goal drains to its already validated safe bay: cancellation does not strand the taxi in a committed turn or halfway through a curb approach. Arrival events identify the actual stop; cancelled/stale goals cannot enable boarding or revive a ride. New validated stop goals can queue behind a current manoeuvre. A request for the same bay currently being entered finishes that single pull-in; a request for the currently occupied bay can revoke a departure that has not started. Direct path replacement remains refused during an authored turn/curb manoeuvre. A departing bay's lease is released only after the pull-out, and retained if it is also the newly queued return target.

Departure waits in the bay for enough directed same-elevation lane space ahead/behind. The rear horizon includes curvature-limited manoeuvre duration from rest, acceleration, headway and the other vehicle's braking distance. Once merging, following uses the same authored path and ambient sensors include the merging body's projected width/length, rather than losing it when its centre leaves a fixed corridor. Different elevations and opposing travel remain filtered. This is a conservative kinematic admission rule, not a fleet planner; persistent busy-lane starvation and narrow geometry still require real traffic testing.

Initial paid distance is **pickup pull-out + forced departure edge + graph route + forced destination approach edge + destination pull-in**, all in logical ground metres. Deadhead to pickup and pickup pull-in are excluded. The fare formula, default/district rate, rounding, generosity tip, bonus tip and one-time settlement remain. The existing additive mid-ride destination-change policy is retained: original planned distance plus the new remaining directed route/maneuvers. Revoking an unstarted departure to remain in the same bay adds zero physical distance. This is not a redesign of fare policy. Historical pickup ID/quote survives pickup disable/destruction after boarding; only the current destination binding governs arrival/settlement. Invalid pre-boarding bindings cancel safely.

Failed navigation returns false without replacing the active request/fare. Actual authoring invalidation during motion freezes physical travel and reports failure rather than teleporting. The pose/lease remain protected; restoring invalid scene authoring while actors are moving is unsupported. Revalidate with stopped traffic/restart the fixture after authoring corrections. Rejected visitors (oversized vehicle or wrong network) cannot invalidate another owner's prepared geometry or lease: validation commits cached geometry transactionally.

## Exact manual authoring specification

Perform these steps in Unity 6000.6.0f1, save normal Unity assets through the Editor, and review the resulting scene/asset diff. Do not generate bays, streets or buildings or write raw scene YAML.

1. Select at least **three** real passenger locations with usable directed road connectivity and off-lane space for meaningful varied offers. At least two reachable bays are the minimum for a fare, but the production offer-board/acceptance expectations need enough passengers/schedules/pairs to sustain multiple visible offers and two consecutive rides.
2. Establish their **registry ownership**. Put the TaxiStopAnchor objects and associated CityLocationAnchor objects under the intended CityWorldRegistry in the hierarchy (with no intervening nested registry). Preserve existing LocationId, PassengerId, district identity, and asset GUIDs. The registry's RoadNetwork must be the one containing every referenced road node.
3. On each selected existing straight drivable street, manually place/choose **non-intersection** approach and departure navigation nodes. Do not place stops at an existing junction just because a location previously used that node. Add only the necessary sparse navigation access nodes on the already handcrafted lane; preserve legal connections/elevations. Endpoints cannot be signal-controlled conflict nodes.
4. Assign TaxiStopAnchor Inspector relationships:

| Field | Inspector assignment / constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Stop Id | Unique stable opaque ID, e.g. `stop_<existing_location_id>_01`; no new canonical place name |
| Location | Existing CityLocationAnchor owned by this registry |
| Approach From Node → Approach Node | Actual directed incoming graph edge; Location.AccessRoadNode must equal Approach Node |
| Departure Node → Departure Next Node | Actual directed outgoing graph edge used after leaving the bay |
| Entry | Physical incoming lane pose at ApproachNode + right(incoming) × ApproachNode.LaneOffsetMeters |
| Pull In First/Second Handle | Manually placed cubic handles, clear of actual sidewalks/buildings/traffic; entry tangent follows incoming lane |
| Stop Pose | Actual centre of taxi fully inside a drawn/recessed off-lane bay |
| Heading Marker | Position ahead of Stop Pose in its intended final ground heading; final pull-in and initial pull-out tangents agree |
| Pull Out First/Second Handle | Manually designed legal departure manoeuvre, starting at the same Stop Pose |
| Exit | Physical lane pose at DepartureNode + right(outgoing) × DepartureNextNode.LaneOffsetMeters; tangent follows outgoing edge |
| Bay Width / Length | Defaults 2.6 m / 6 m; at least actual vehicle width/length plus twice Body Clearance |
| Body Clearance | Default 0.25 m; tune deliberately for actual envelope/scene, not to bypass an invalid bay |
| Through Lane Width | Default 3 m; use actual street class width when validating stationary body separation |

5. Determine **real ground positions in the Editor**, using the existing IsometricGroundProjection/Grid conventions. The defaults are width 2 m/length 4.5 m for a taxi; CarModelSO adds WidthMeters and CurbSpeed (default 2 m/s), while acceleration/braking/lateral acceleration retain their existing tuning. Inspect the actual assigned model; demand initially filters for the nominal 4.5 × 2 m taxi and activation revalidates its real dimensions.
6. Make the complete **stationary body** clear of every same-elevation directed lane strip plus margin. Both full manoeuvres must clear every conflict region by radius + body half-diagonal + margin. The cached 32 intervals and conservative body checks do not establish the real drivable surface or prove a swept-body fit between samples. Inspect the full car envelope continuously. Add actual authored bay recess/curb/frontage visuals without consuming required clear sidewalk space. A logical anchor alone does not create a safe bay.
7. The C# test fixture is a **reference for component relationships, not production coordinates**: lane y=-1.5; entry (20,-1.5), handles (24,-1.5)/(26,-5), stop (30,-5), out handles (34,-5)/(36,-1.5), exit (40,-1.5), heading marker (31,-5). It models two 10.737955 m cubics, a 2.6 × 6 m bay and directed road access. Do not copy it into the city without checking actual curb/building/intersection geometry.
8. Update location AccessRoadNode and WorldLocationSO.ConnectedRoadNodeId coherently. Update the relevant passenger schedule PreferredNodeId to the explicit stop's approach access node, keeping passenger and location IDs unchanged. Demand still uses the existing schedule/time model; it must resolve to a unique authored stop. Rebuild RoadNetwork and CityWorldRegistry through their existing commands; run Validate Taxi Stop and world validation for every bay.
9. Complete Batch A scene integration in the same authoring pass: manually placed RoadTurnPaths for selected actual movements, one complete signalized junction, approach stop lines **upstream of conflict regions and turn entries**, and visible lamps bound to the signal State.
